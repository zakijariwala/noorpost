# Noori's Notebook — the zine programme

Free or near-free. Feeds Instagram, gets handed out at majlis, and travels to the madrasa meeting in the same bag as the sample envelope.

**Events and places only. Never people.** People live in envelopes, and one-person zines would sell the same six people twice.

---

## Format

One A4 sheet. Folded to eight pages. One cut. Single ink. **Prints on equipment you already own.**

### The fold

Standard eight-page zine fold: fold in half three times, unfold, cut the centre slit along the middle horizontal, fold in half lengthwise and push the ends together. Every page is A7.

### The imposition

Pages do not sit in reading order on the sheet. On the flat A4, printed one side only:

```
top row, upside down, left to right:   5   4   3   2
bottom row, right way up, left to right: 6   7   8   1
```

**Print one test sheet, fold it, and read it before laying out anything.** Every zine programme in history has burned a print run on this.

### The eight pages

| Page | Job | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover. The name of the event or place. One image. | ≤ 6 |
| 2 | Where and when. Put it on the map and in the year. | 40–60 |
| 3 | What happened, part one | 50–70 |
| 4 | What happened, part two | 50–70 |
| 5 | The thing most people do not know | 50–70 |
| 6 | What is there now | 40–60 |
| 7 | One thing to do, or one thing to look at | 30–50 |
| 8 | Back cover. Where this came from, and where the rest is. | ≤ 30 |

**Total 300–400 words.** A zine is read standing up, at a majlis, by somebody holding a cup.

## Rules

Everything in `00-foundations/` applies, with these differences:

| Rule | In a zine |
|---|---|
| Two-voice letter | **Not used.** One voice, plain. |
| Death lines | Applies wherever a death is named. Fact and actor, no method. |
| Traditional marker | Applies, verbatim |
| Differ-by-community line | Applies, verbatim |
| Sourcing | Applies in full. **A zine is where a citation is most likely to be dropped for space. Do not drop it.** Page 8 carries it. |
| Superlatives, counts | Banned, same as everywhere |
| Faces | **No faces.** These are places and events. |

## Page 8 always carries

> Sources on the back. Noor Post makes a set of fourteen envelopes about the Fourteen Masoomeen, one for each month.
>
> [source line] · [where to find the rest]

**No selling language.** The zine advertises by being good and by naming what else exists.

## Release timing

Time each release against its date in the calendar where one exists — Ghadir on 18 Dhul Hijjah, Laylat al-Mabit on 1 Muharram, Dahw al-Ard on 25 Dhul Qa'dah, Mubahala on 24 Dhul Hijjah. The dateless ones fill the gaps.

**Ghadir is the pilot zine.** It goes to the madrasa meeting alongside the sample envelope, and it is the one the whole channel test rides on.
