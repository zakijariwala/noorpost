# Zine 03 — Mubahala

**Release:** 24 Dhul Hijjah

---

## PAGE 1 — Cover

> # MUBAHALA
>
> *An argument that did not happen*

Image: two groups facing each other across open ground, single ink. No figures — tents, or footprints in sand, standing in for the people.

---

## PAGE 2 — Where and when

> Medina, 10 AH. A delegation travels from Najran, far to the south, to debate a question with the Prophet that neither side is willing to give up on.
>
> Days pass. Both sides argue the same ground over and over, and nobody moves.

---

## PAGE 3 — What happened, part one

> Eventually a different kind of proposal is put forward, one that ends an argument instead of continuing it: since words are not settling it, let both sides bring their families — the people each side loves most — and stand together, and let whichever side is wrong bear the consequence together, in front of the other.
>
> It is agreed to. A morning is set.

---

## PAGE 4 — What happened, part two

> The morning comes. Each side is meant to bring who matters most to them.
>
> The Prophet brings his own household — his daughter, her husband, their two sons. Not an army, not a crowd of supporters. Four people.

---

## PAGE 5 — The thing most people do not know

> **It never took place.**
>
> The delegation looks at who has actually been brought — not soldiers, not scholars, a small family standing together — and something about the seriousness of that stops them. They withdraw the challenge before it starts.
>
> A treaty is agreed instead. **The famous event is the one that was called off**, and it is remembered for who showed up to it, not for what happened next.

---

## PAGE 6 — What is there now

> Najran is a real place today, on the Saudi side of the border with Yemen. The treaty terms from that meeting are recorded and survive in the historical record — what was agreed, and what each side owed the other.

---

## PAGE 7 — One thing to do

> **Find an argument in this house that stopped because somebody was willing to stake something real on it.**
>
> Not who won. The moment it stopped — because someone showed they meant it enough to risk something that mattered to them, and that changed what the argument was about.

## Production notes

**Page 4 names nobody outright.** "His daughter, her husband, their two sons" is accurate and matches how the outline itself leaves it — the identification is well attested but the wording is flagged for the scholar per `outlines.md`, and this draft does not pre-empt that review by asserting names as settled house style.

**Page 5 carries the whole zine.** The mubahala is famous as an event, but the actual content is an event that was withdrawn from — that reversal is the "one new thing" and page 5 is where it has to land clearly.
