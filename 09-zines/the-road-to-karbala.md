# Zine 08 — The road to Karbala

**Muharram**

---

## PAGE 1 — Cover

> # THE ROAD TO KARBALA
>
> *Eight hundred miles*

Image: a single road running to the horizon, single ink. No figures.

---

## PAGE 2 — Where and when

> Makkah to Kufa, the long way, in autumn. A household sets out — not an army — travelling toward a city that had written asking for them.

---

## PAGE 3 — What happened, part one

> The journey runs as a route: stops, days on the road, places where people joined along the way and places where people turned back once the risk became clear.

---

## PAGE 4 — What happened, part two

> Further along, the road is blocked, and the destination changes because of who is blocking it and where.

---

## PAGE 5 — The thing most people do not know

> **They were turned aside, not stopped at a chosen destination.**
>
> Karbala was not where anyone had set out for. It is a place picked by whoever controlled the road at that point — flat, off the main route, with a river nearby and no town to shelter in. **The place became famous. It was never the plan.**

---

## PAGE 6 — What is there now

> The route is walked today by extraordinary numbers of people every year at Arbaeen — on foot, over days, retracing a version of the same ground.

---

## PAGE 7 — One thing to do

> **Put the route on a map at home.**
>
> Find Makkah and Kufa, and trace the rough distance between them. Then measure it against the longest journey your own family has made, and see how it compares.

## Production notes

**Ends before Ashura, same rule as envelope 01.** This zine is the journey, not the day it ended on — page 4 stops at the road being blocked, not at what followed. No scene of the day itself belongs in this object.

**Page 5 is the whole point of writing this as a road zine rather than a battle zine** — most retellings start at the destination; this one insists the destination was not the plan.
