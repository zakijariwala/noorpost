## Two rules that protect the flagship

> ### No hadith card. No event print. Ever.

Those two carry the silsila and the calendar ring. The moment a bonus envelope holds one, the box stops being the only way to complete anything.

This is not a guideline. It is the reason the line is safe to sell at all, and it should be enforced in the template rather than remembered per envelope.

## You can draw their faces

**Use it.** It is a difference a child sees instantly and the Fourteen will never allow. The person print in this line is a portrait of a person, not a building.

## Every envelope points home

The connection to the Fourteen prints on each one, as a fact, with no selling language.

**The first six all pointed to just two of the Fourteen — the Prophet and Imam Ali.** The four added after them were chosen specifically to reach Masoomeen the line had no presence for at all: al-Mahdi, Husayn, Fatima, al-Baqir. Ten companions now still leave eight Masoomeen (Hasan, al-Sajjad, al-Kadhim, al-Rida, al-Askari, al-Hadi, al-Sadiq, al-Jawad) without one — worth closing further before treating this line as balanced.

## What carries over from the Fourteen
