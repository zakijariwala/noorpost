ALI IBN MUHAMMAD AL-SAMARRI

●Who he served. Fourth and final of the trusted agents (sufara') of Imam al-Mahdi, named by Husayn ibn Ruh before his death.

●How long he held it. The shortest of the four terms — only a few years, by most accounts, before his own death closed the arrangement.

●What he received. A final letter (tawqi'), close to his own death, instructing him not to name a successor and instructing the community not to accept anyone who claimed the post afterward.

●What that meant. The end of an arrangement that had run, one man to the next, for about seventy years — ended in writing, on purpose, not left to trail off unexplained.

●Why he is in this envelope. Ali was the fourth of the four, and the last letter he ever received said there would not be a fifth.

He died shortly after, and no fifth deputy was ever named.
