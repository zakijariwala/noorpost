HAMIDA KHATUN

●Who she was. Wife of Imam al-Sadiq and mother of Imam al-Kadhim.

●What she is remembered for. Being formally trusted by Imam al-Sadiq to teach matters of religion to women sent to her directly, rather than to him.

●What that shows. A recognised authority in her own right within the household, not only a caretaker of it — knowledge and care held together rather than kept separate.

●Her son. Raised in a household where his mother was known, publicly, as someone other women were sent to learn from.

●Why she is in this envelope. Hamida did not just raise Imam al-Kadhim — Imam al-Sadiq himself sent women to be taught by her.

She lived to see her son recognised as Imam after his father's death.
