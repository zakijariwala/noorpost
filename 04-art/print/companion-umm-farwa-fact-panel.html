UMM FARWA

●Who she was. Mother of Imam al-Sadiq, from a family with its own line of learning and standing.

●What is remembered. Imam al-Sadiq named her specifically among a small group of women he described in terms of faith and character.

●What that shows. A precise, considered assessment rather than a generic tribute — the kind of praise that has to be earned rather than simply offered.

●Her household. Known for learning within her own family line, carried into how she raised her son.

●Why she is in this envelope. Imam al-Sadiq said his mother was among the believing women — and named her specifically, rather than praising mothers in general.

She lived to see her son become one of the most widely followed teachers of his generation.
