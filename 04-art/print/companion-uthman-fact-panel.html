UTHMAN IBN SA'ID AL-AMRI

●His trade. An oil seller in Samarra and later Baghdad — a real trade, worked for real, that also happened to be a cover nobody thought twice about.

●Who he served. Trusted agent (wakil) to Imam al-Hadi, then to Imam al-Askari — carrying funds and messages between the Imam and a community that could not risk being seen visiting him directly.

●What came after. When Imam al-Askari died, Uthman became the first of the four men who carried messages for Imam al-Mahdi during the years that followed. First of four, and already decades into the same work when he started.

●Why he was chosen again and again. Not on reputation alone — each appointment came after years already served, watched and tested, under the Imam before.

●Why he is in this envelope. Uthman carried messages for three Imams in a row, ending with Imam al-Mahdi.

He died in Baghdad, still in the role, and named the next man himself before he did.
