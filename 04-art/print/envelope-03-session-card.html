Talk about it

About ten minutes. No right answers, and no points.

1. The families had been building together all week. What changed when they got to the stone?

●Grown-up answers this one first.

2. Tell about a time you wanted to be the one who did something, and someone else did it instead. What did you do?

3. In the letter you said whether you would have given it up. Now that you know how it ended — say it again. Same answer?

4. He could have carried the stone himself. Everyone would have let him. Why do you think he didn't?

⚑This one is hard for a grown-up.

5. They trusted him before he had done anything for them that day. What had he been doing for the twenty years before it?

Last: find something in your house that two people have to carry.
