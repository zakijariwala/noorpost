Envelope 12 · Shawwal · Conversation

Talk about it

About ten minutes. No right answers, and no points.

1. People told him to raise an army and he opened a class instead. Which one lasts longer?

2. ● Grown-up answers this one first. Name a teacher you had whose name you still remember, and say what you got from them.

3. His students disagreed with each other, sometimes badly, and he taught them anyway. Should he have?

4. One of them spent his life on what happens when you mix things. Nobody had a word for it yet. What are people doing now that has no word for it yet?

5. ⚑ This one is hard for a grown-up. He got about ten free years and used them on something that paid nothing back in his lifetime. What are you doing with yours?

Last: Teach each other something. Five minutes each. ● goes second.
