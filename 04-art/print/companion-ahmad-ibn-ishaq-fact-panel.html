AHMAD IBN ISHAQ AL-QUMMI

●His role. A trusted representative of the Qom community, carrying questions to Imam al-Askari and returning with answers, repeatedly over years.

●Why it mattered. This kind of relay work was one of the few reliable channels between a watched household and a community that could not simply visit.

●What he was shown. By some accounts, given a rare, quiet glimpse of the Imam's young son during a period when the household was closely watched.

●Why it mattered further. A small, carefully placed act of trust, at a time when trust itself was dangerous to extend.

●Why he is in this envelope. Ahmad carried his city's questions to Imam al-Askari for years, and was shown something almost no one else was.

Little else of his later life connects directly to this account.
