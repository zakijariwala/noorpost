SAFWAN AL-JAMMAL

●His trade. A camel merchant, hiring out transport — ordinary, respectable, unremarkable work.

●The order. Asked to supply camels for the ruling caliph's own journey — profitable, routine business by any normal measure.

●The question. Imam al-Kadhim asked whether he would be sorry to lose the ruler as a customer — surfacing, gently, what the business actually rested on.

●What he did. Sold his entire fleet of camels rather than keep his income tied, even indirectly, to the ruler he served.

●Why he is in this envelope. Safwan turned down profitable work because of who it would help, and Imam al-Kadhim told him he had done right.

Little else of his later life is recorded in detail.
