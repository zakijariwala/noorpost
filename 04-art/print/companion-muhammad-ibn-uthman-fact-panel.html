MUHAMMAD IBN UTHMAN AL-AMRI

●His trade. An oil seller, like his father before him — the same market, the same cover, carried on rather than invented fresh.

●Who he served. Second of the four trusted agents (sufara') of Imam al-Mahdi, named to the post by his father, Uthman ibn Sa'id, before Uthman died.

●How long he held it. About forty years — the longest term of the four, by a wide margin, spanning most of the arrangement's whole seventy-year span.

●What came after. Before his own death, he named Husayn ibn Ruh as the third of the four, the same way his father had named him.

●Why he is in this envelope. Muhammad kept his father's post for about forty years, and never once let the family trade stop being the cover it was.

He died still holding the post, having named his successor himself.
