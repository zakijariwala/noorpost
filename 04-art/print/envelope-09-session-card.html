Envelope 09 · Sha'ban · Conversation

Talk about it

About ten minutes. No right answers, and no points.

1. He says you owe your tongue something. What do you think you owe it?

2. ● Grown-up answers this one first. There is an entry for what a parent owes a child. Say one thing you think you owe this child, out loud, now.

3. The hardest entry is the one about a person who has wronged you. What could you possibly owe them?

4. He wrote what you owe, not what you are owed. Why do you think he did it that way round?

5. ⚑ This one is hard for a grown-up. Which entry on that list are you currently not paying?

Last: Add one entry the list does not have. Both of you write it. Keep it in the box.
