Envelope 10 · Sha'ban · Open

Write one

No questions this time. No answer card. Nothing to get right.

For seventy years, people wrote things down and handed them over, and did not know when the answer would come, or whether it would come in their lifetime.

Both of you write one. ● writes one. ○ writes one. Not to each other.

Anything. A question. A complaint. Something you want. Something you would only say if you were sure nobody was going to reply this week.

Do not read each other's.

Fold them. Put them in this envelope. Seal it with the sticker.

Write next Sha'ban's date on the front.

Then put it away, and do not open it for a year.

That is the whole activity. It is meant to feel unfinished.
