Envelope 11 · Ramadan · Case File

The question

What was a caliph with an army actually afraid of?

The five evidence cards

EVIDENCE 1 The search was unannounced and at night. Nothing could have been prepared or hidden in advance.

EVIDENCE 2 They found no weapons, no plans, no money for an army, and nobody gathered. The soldiers reported that back.

EVIDENCE 3 After the search he was not released from attention. He was moved to Samarra, the garrison city, and kept there for about twenty years.

EVIDENCE 4 He had no army, no province, no treasury and no title from the state. Nothing that could be confiscated.

EVIDENCE 5 People travelled to Samarra to be near him from all over the empire, at their own expense, and got nothing material in return for going.
