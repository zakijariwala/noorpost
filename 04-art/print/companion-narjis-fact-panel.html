NARJIS KHATUN

●Who she was. Wife of Imam al-Askari, and mother of Imam al-Mahdi.

●What she is remembered for. Carrying her pregnancy with great secrecy, in a household under close watch by a government wary of exactly this birth.

●What that required. Sustained discretion over months, within a small circle of trust, in a house that could not risk drawing outside attention to itself.

●What this letter does not do. Explain the occultation itself, or what it means — that belongs to envelope-10.md, held to the same restraint.

●Why she is in this envelope. Narjis kept a birth secret for months, in a house being watched, and told almost no one until it was already over.

Accounts of her later life vary and are held here loosely, per the same practice as the fact panel for Hakima Khatun in envelope 10.
