HUSAYN IBN RUH AL-NAWBAKHTI

●His background. Born into the Nawbakhti family, well known in Baghdad for scholarship — unlike the first two deputies, he was a public figure before he ever took the post.

●Who he served. Third of the four trusted agents (sufara') of Imam al-Mahdi, named by Muhammad ibn Uthman before his death.

●What he was known for. Extraordinary discretion under direct questioning — reports describe interrogators coming away satisfied without having learned anything usable.

●What it cost him. Imprisoned more than once during his term, by rulers who suspected the role without ever being able to prove it.

●Why he is in this envelope. Husayn served the third of the four terms, and was so careful with what he said that even his enemies could not catch him saying too much.

He died still holding the post, having named Ali ibn Muhammad al-Samarri as the fourth.
