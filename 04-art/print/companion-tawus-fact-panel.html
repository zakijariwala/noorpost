TAWUS AL-YAMANI

●Who he was. A respected early scholar from Yemen, known in his own right for learning, not primarily as anyone's attendant.

●What he witnessed. Imam al-Sajjad's private night prayer near the Kaaba — long, repeated, unrequested, and largely unseen by the wider community.

●Why it is remembered. Because it was unwitnessed by design. Nothing about the practice changed whether or not anyone was present to see it.

●What he did with it. Kept the account and passed it on carefully enough that it is still told today.

●Why he is in this envelope. Tawus watched Imam al-Sajjad pray, alone, all night, for years, without ever being asked to.

Little else of his life connects directly to this account.
