DIBIL AL-KHUZA'I

●His craft. A poet, known and invited for his skill — poetry recited aloud, in company, remembered and repeated afterward.

●The poem. A long composition on the history of the Prophet's family, direct rather than guarded in what it named.

●Where he recited it. In the presence of Imam al-Rida, at real personal risk given the content and the setting.

●What followed. The Imam heard it through and gave Dibil his own cloak as a gift, in recognition of it.

●Why he is in this envelope. Dibil recited a poem in front of Imam al-Rida that most people were too afraid to say out loud.

Little else of his later life connects directly to this account.
