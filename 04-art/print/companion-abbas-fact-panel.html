ABBAS IBN ALI

●Who he was. Son of Imam Ali and Umm al-Banin, and Imam Husayn's half-brother. Grew up close to the family he would stay beside until the end.

●His role at Karbala. Standard-bearer — the one who carried the flag, and the one sent when something dangerous needed doing and trust mattered more than rank.

●The water. On the final days, the camp's access to the river was cut off. Abbas broke through to it, filled a waterskin for the children, and turned back without drinking himself.

●Why he is remembered for it. Not the fighting alone — the choice, at the water's edge, thirsty himself, to carry it to others first.

●Why he is in this envelope. Abbas carried water toward the camp, for the children, at Karbala.

He was killed at Karbala, on the day of Ashura, on his way back to the tents with the water.
