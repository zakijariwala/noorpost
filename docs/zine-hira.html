Hira

One sheet, folded to eight pages.

Page 1 · Cover

# HIRA

A hole in a mountain

Page 2 · Where and when

A cave near the top of a mountain called Jabal al-Nur, about two miles from the Kaaba. Not a cave you walk into. A crack in the rock at the top of a steep climb that takes most people well over half an hour and leaves them out of breath.

It is small. A few people can sit in it. From inside, the opening frames the valley and the city below.

Page 3 · What happened, part one

Before anything else happened there, it was where a man went to be alone.

He went for stretches at a time, taking food and water up with him, and came back down when it ran out. He had been doing this for years before the year everyone remembers. Years of climbing a mountain to sit in a hole in it and think.

He was in his late thirties. He had a family, a house and a trade.

Page 4 · What happened, part two

Then, in one of those stretches, in the month of Ramadan, something happened in that cave that had not happened to anybody before.

He was told to read. He said he could not. It happened again, and again, and then the first words of the Qur'an were given to him.

And he went down the mountain frightened, and went home, and was believed by his wife before he was believed by anybody else on earth.

Page 5 · The thing most people do not know

The cave faces the Kaaba.

Sit inside it and the opening looks out over the valley, and the Kaaba is in that view.

So the years of going up there alone were not years of getting away from the city. He climbed above it, and sat, and looked straight down at the thing everyone else was arguing about — and at a building that had been full of idols for as long as anyone could remember.

Page 6 · What is there now

The mountain is still there and people still climb it, in enormous numbers, in the heat, in flip-flops.

There is a path now, and steps in places, and people selling water on the way up. The cave itself is unchanged and still too small for the queue outside it.

It is the one place in this series you can still stand inside exactly as it was.

Page 7 · One thing to look at

Find out how long half an hour of climbing is.

Not reading about it — doing it. Stairs, a hill, anything with height in it. Go up until you have to stop, then work out how much further you would have had to go.

He did that carrying food, in that heat, repeatedly, for years, before anything happened at all.

The famous part took one night. The unfamous part took years.
