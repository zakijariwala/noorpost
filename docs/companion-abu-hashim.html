Asked Before He Could Ask

● the grown-up  ·  ○ the child  ·  ●○ together

●Abu Hashim served Imam al-Hadi for years — the kind of long, ordinary, unglamorous loyalty that doesn't come with any title attached to it at all.

○Was he paid well for that kind of service?

●Not especially, no, not in any way that made his life easier. He was often short of money, the plain, quiet kind of short that means simply going without, not the dramatic kind of thing you mention easily to anyone at all.

○Did he ever ask the Imam for help?

●No, never, not once in all those years. He kept it entirely to himself, the way people often do when asking out loud starts to feel like admitting something uncomfortable about yourself.

○Then how did the Imam even come to know?

●That's the part people still tell, generations later, almost like a small miracle wrapped inside an ordinary day. Before Abu Hashim ever said a single word about it, provisions simply arrived for him — enough to cover exactly what he needed, at exactly the time he needed it most.

○But how would the Imam have known, if he never said anything at all?

●Nobody has a tidy answer for that, and honestly, the account never tries hard to supply one. It simply says, plainly, that it happened, and that it happened more than once.

○Did Abu Hashim ever ask how the Imam knew?

●If he did, it isn't recorded anywhere at all. He seems to have simply taken it as it came, and stayed exactly as quiet about his needs afterward as he had always, carefully, been before.

○Is there something you need but never said? Say it now, out loud.

●Most people are quietly carrying at least one thing exactly like that, whether they would ever admit it or not.

●○He never asked, and somehow, every time, he never quite had to.

Letter, reverse

ABU HASHIM AL-JA'FARI

●His service. A long-standing, trusted companion across more than one imamate, including years of service to Imam al-Hadi.

●His circumstances. Often in real financial need, which he did not raise directly.

●What is recorded. By multiple accounts, provisions or support reaching him from the Imam without his having asked, more than once.

●Why it is remembered. Not as a miracle story dwelt on for its mystery, but as a fact about being known and looked after without asking — the register this whole line stays inside.

●Why he is in this envelope. Abu Hashim never asked Imam al-Hadi for anything, and the Imam sent help before he had to.

Little else of his later life connects directly to this account.
