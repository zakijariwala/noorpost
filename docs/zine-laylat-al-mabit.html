Page 1 · Cover

# LAYLAT AL-MABIT

The night somebody else slept in the bed

Page 2 · Where and when

Makkah, the night before the Hijra. The house is being watched by men who intend to act against the Prophet at first light.

Page 3 · What happened, part one

The plan: the Prophet leaves the house under cover of night. Ali stays behind and lies down in his place, wrapped in his cloak, so that from outside the house looks exactly as it should — occupied, undisturbed, nothing wrong.

Page 4 · What happened, part two

Morning comes. The men who had been watching all night go in expecting to find one person, and find another.

Page 5 · The thing most people do not know

The plan only works if he lies completely still.

Not a fight. Not a chase. Hours of holding position in the dark, knowing exactly who and what is outside the door, doing nothing at all except staying where he is.

It is the hardest kind of courage to draw, and the easiest to explain to a child — because every child already knows what it costs to lie still and quiet when they are frightened.

Page 6 · What is there now

The house itself does not survive. The road the Prophet took out of Makkah that night does, and is part of the broader story of the Hijra told across the tradition.

Page 7 · One thing to do

Lie completely still for three minutes.

No phone, no talking, eyes open or closed, just still. Time it. Then imagine doing that for an entire night, knowing people outside meant you harm.
