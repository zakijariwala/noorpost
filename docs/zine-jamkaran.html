Jamkaran

One sheet, folded to eight pages.

Page 1 · Cover

# JAMKARAN

A mosque built because of a dream

Page 2 · Where and when

A village outside Qom, over a thousand years ago — long enough back that the story of how the mosque came to be built is itself part of the place.

Page 3 · What happened, part one

Traditionally told: a man is visited in a dream and told to go to the landowner and ask that a mosque be built on a particular spot, with specific instructions about where and how.

Page 4 · What happened, part two

It is built. And people keep coming — for a thousand years, without a gap, the site has stayed in use and in memory.

Page 5 · The thing most people do not know

The instructions in the account are oddly practical.

Not a vision of light or a symbol to interpret — where to build, how, and what to do there once it stood. A brief, not a vision. That plainness is part of why the account has been retold so consistently for so long.

Page 6 · What is there now

The mosque is now large, and busiest on Tuesday nights, when it draws crowds well beyond what the original building could hold. It has been rebuilt and expanded many times since.

Page 7 · One thing to do

Write a note and keep it.

People who visit Jamkaran often write something down — a request, a hope — and leave it there. You don't need to travel there to try the actual practice: write one down, and keep it somewhere you'll find it again in a year.
