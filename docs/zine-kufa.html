Kufa

One sheet, folded to eight pages.

Page 1 · Cover

# KUFA

The city that could not decide

Page 2 · Where and when

A garrison town in Iraq, founded new, laid out on a grid, built to house soldiers. Not an old city grown up over centuries — a planned one, built fast, for a purpose.

Page 3 · What happened, part one

It becomes Imam Ali's capital, and the reasons are practical rather than symbolic — its position, its population, what it offered a government that needed to be somewhere central.

Page 4 · What happened, part two

What happens there afterwards is told flatly here and nowhere dressed up: a city that supported, then hesitated, then failed to act, more than once, when it mattered most.

Page 5 · The thing most people do not know

It was a new town full of people from everywhere else.

Nobody's family had lived there for generations, because there was no "there" before the town was built. No old loyalties, no long memory holding people together. That explains a great deal of what Kufa did, and failed to do, when it counted.

Page 6 · What is there now

Kufa is part of Najaf governorate today, and the historic masjid associated with Imam Ali still stands and is in active use.

Page 7 · One thing to do

Find out how old your own town or neighbourhood is, and who built the oldest thing still standing in it.

A place with deep roots behaves differently from a place without them. See if you can tell which kind of place you live in.
