# Standard lines

Fixed wordings. Typed once, reused verbatim, never paraphrased. A rule that is reworded in each envelope stops being a rule and becomes a tone.

---

## 1. Where communities differ

Printed wherever a date, an observance or a practice is not held the same way across communities.

> **Communities observe this differently. Ask your family which we follow.**

**Set in the parent's register but printed in the open, where both will see it.** The point is that the child hears the question handed over.

Do not soften it. Do not add "some say" or "in our tradition". The line works because it declines to answer and says who does.

**Where it is used, project-wide:**

| Envelope | What differs |
|---|---|
| 02 | Imam Hasan's shahadat — 7th or 28th Safar |
| 03 | The Prophet's birth — 12th or 17th Rabi al-Awwal |
| 03 | The Prophet's death — 28 Safar or 12 Rabi al-Awwal |
| 05 | Imam al-Baqir's month, if the 05/11 swap stays open |
| 09 | The night of Munajat Sha'baniyya |
| 11 | Laylat al-Qadr — which night |
| 11 | Imam al-Hadi's month, if the 05/11 swap stays open |

## 2. Traditional accounts

Printed against any account that is loved but not documented. **In the child's copy, not only the parent's.**

Long form, where the account carries a whole item:

> **This story is loved and retold, and we do not have it written down from the time it happened. We have told you which parts are which.**

Short form, inline in a fact panel bullet, set in italic after the claim:

> *Traditionally told.*

**Where it is used, project-wide:**

| Envelope | What is marked |
|---|---|
| 06 | Fizza answering in Qur'anic verse for about twenty years |
| 06 | Bayt al-Ahzan |
| 09 | Shahrbanu's origin |
| 10 | Hakima Khatun present at the birth |
| 07 | Birth inside the Kaaba — **ask the scholar.** Widely held and widely attested in Shia sources; the marker may read as doubt where none is intended. |

The distinction being drawn is documentation, not truth. Say it that way in the parent's copy if it needs saying: *We mark what is written down from the time and what is not. Both can be true.*

## 3. The Masoom's name

First mention in any item carries the full form. After that, the short form.

| Full, first mention | Short, thereafter |
|---|---|
| The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his family) | The Prophet |
| Imam Ali (peace be upon him) | Imam Ali |
| Sayyida Fatima al-Zahra (peace be upon her) | Sayyida Fatima |
| Imam Hasan (peace be upon him) | Imam Hasan |
| Imam Husayn (peace be upon him) | Imam Husayn |
| Imam al-Sajjad (peace be upon him) | Imam al-Sajjad |
| Imam al-Baqir, and so on | As above |

**In child lines, the short form always.** The honorific is carried by the adult voice.

Never "he" as the first word of an item. A child picking a card off the floor six months later needs to know who it is about.

---

## 4. The return postcard

Pre-addressed. Two signature lines. Fixed wording on the back:

> We opened this one together.
>
> ● ______________________  ○ ______________________
>
> *Post it back to us, or keep it. Either is right.*

**Two signature lines, both filled or neither.** The postcard is the only object in the envelope that records the twenty-five minutes actually happened.

---

## 5. Inside the flap

Every envelope, same block, same position:

> **Open together. About twenty-five minutes.**
>
> 1. The letter — read it out loud, ● and ○ taking turns
> 2. The hadith card
> 3. The prints
> 4. [Session type]
> 5. The stickers
> 6. The postcard
>
> ● is the grown-up. ○ is you.

Mourning issues replace item 5 with *The pennant* and carry one extra line:

> *This one has no game in it.*

---

## 6. Sourcing credit

Foot of the fact panel, small:

> [Work], [book/section] [number]. Translated by [translator], [publisher] [year].

Hadith card reverse, smaller still, under the silsila segment:

> [Work] [number] · trans. [translator]

Never cite without a number. Never cite a translation without naming the translator.

---

## 7. Lines that are banned

| Never printed | Why |
|---|---|
| "This teaches us that…" | The session card asks. The letter does not answer. |
| "Like the Imam, we should…" | Instructional register. Turns the envelope into homework. |
| "Sadly," / "Tragically," | Tells the reader what to feel. |
| "Little did he know…" | Builds to the death. Rule L14. |
| Any count of followers, converts or people influenced | Rule F2. Does not survive checking. |
| "The greatest / the most important / the best" | Same. |
| "Martyred" in a child line | Adult lines only. |
